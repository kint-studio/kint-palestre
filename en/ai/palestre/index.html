SERVICES

* Website

“Your gym”

A fast website designed for the phone, where people looking for a gym find classes, schedules and prices and contact you with a tap.

  • Classes, schedules and prices always up to date, with one message from you
  • Presentation of your spaces, instructors and the style of the gym
  • Contact or trial request with a tap, including on WhatsApp
  • In Italian, German and English, with a map and opening hours

* Membership management

“Members manage themselves”

Sign-ups, renewals and expiry dates online, with no queues at reception and no spreadsheets. You keep everything under control in one place.

  • Sign-up and membership renewal straight from the website
  • Plans and prices always up to date and clear
  • Automatic reminders before expiry
  • List of members and expiry dates in one place
  • Integration with the management software you already use, to be assessed together

* Social media

“Workouts that motivate”

Instagram and Facebook run by people who do this for a living, with photos and reels of your classes, your spaces and your community.

  • Strategy and post calendar, agreed with you
  • Photos and reels at the gym, with dedicated shoots
  • Stories, replies to comments and messages
  • Local Meta campaigns for free trials and new classes

* Google and visibility

“They find you when they look for you”

When someone searches for “gym near me”, you need to be there, with photos, opening hours and reviews in order.

  • Optimised Google Business Profile, with photos, opening hours and a contact button
  • Reviews: automatic requests and AI-assisted replies, checked by a person
  • Local SEO for people searching in your area, in German and English too
  • Visibility on ChatGPT and AI Overviews, monthly report

* AI and automation

“Repetitive work, on autopilot”

Chatbots and automatic workflows that answer people asking for information and take care of repetitive reception tasks, even outside opening hours.

  • Website chatbot that answers questions on classes, schedules and prices, even when the gym is closed
  • Trial requests collected and confirmed automatically
  • Automatic welcome message for new members, with schedules and classes
  • Together we pick the repetitive tasks worth automating, starting with the most useful

FAQ

Q: How much does it cost?

A: Fixed prices, no surprises. On the call, after seeing your gym, we tell you what it costs and how long it takes, before you sign anything.

Q: Do I have to take all the services?

A: No. We start with what you need most, for example the website with membership management, and add the rest whenever you like.

Q: How does membership management work?

A: Your members choose and renew their membership on the website, and you keep members and expiry dates under control in one place. If you already use management software or an access system, we'll talk about it on the call.

Q: Can I update classes and schedules myself?

A: You can, but you don't have to: send us a message with the new schedule or the new class and we'll update it.

Q: Do you run the social media?

A: Yes: post calendar, photos and reels of your gym, and replies to messages. We agree everything with you, and nothing goes online without a person having reviewed it.

Q: How long does it take to go online?

A: About ten working days from the call. Most of the time goes into collecting photos and opening hours, which is why we only ask you for a ten-minute form.
